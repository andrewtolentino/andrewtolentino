After 14+ years in product design—and the last several managing design teams—I left to do the work I kept wishing existed when I was navigating my own career.

I'm the founder of Honest Club, a coaching practice and community for product designers who need more than generic career advice. I work with career changers, designers from non-traditional backgrounds, and ICs making the move into management. My approach is simple: teach the fundamentals, then help you apply them to your reality—because the advice isn't wrong, it's just incomplete.

I'm a Co-Active Practitioner (CAP) trained through CTI, working toward full ICF accreditation.
